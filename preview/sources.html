Rules the gate enforces
  • robots.txt disallow means no collection — permanently, until the registry changes.
  • Sources requiring authentication are never crawled with credentials.
  • A policy review older than 365 days blocks the source automatically.
  • Licensed feeds carry redistribution_allowed=false until a written right exists.
  • Captchas are never solved; a captcha-gated source stays blocked.

Every figure carries its source. Prices are shown with their basis — awarded, L1/L2/L3 quote, estimated or market — and bases are never blended in a statistic.

Individual officer names are not searchable or exportable. Tender documents remain the property of the issuing department; this platform shows extracted facts and links to the origin.
