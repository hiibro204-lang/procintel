How your key is handled
  • Validated before it is saved.
    We ask the provider who the key belongs to. A rejected key is never stored.
  • Encrypted at rest.
    Fernet encryption with a key held outside the database. A dump or backup does not expose it.
  • Never returned.
    The API only ever emits the masked form. Nothing logs the key.
  • Effective immediately.
    The next extraction or question uses it. No restart.

Collection policy

What the platform is allowed to collect is set by the source registry, not by this page. Each source carries its robots status, terms summary, rate limit and review date, and a connector will not run without a current record.

Review sources →

Every figure carries its source. Prices are shown with their basis — awarded, L1/L2/L3 quote, estimated or market — and bases are never blended in a statistic.

Individual officer names are not searchable or exportable. Tender documents remain the property of the issuing department; this platform shows extracted facts and links to the origin.
